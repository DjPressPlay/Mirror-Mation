Welcome to AI GIF Remixer!

Transform any existing GIF into a new AI-generated looping masterpiece

1 Search for a GIF

Click "Start Remixing" in the sidebar and search Giphy for a GIF you want to transform.

2 Select Your Source

Pick a GIF to use as your reference. The app will extract frames and analyze the motion.

3 Adjust Settings

Use the controls to adjust motion intensity, smoothness, speed, and choose a style preset.

4 Generate Remix

Click Generate to create AI-powered remixed frames that match the vibe and loop logic of the original.

5 Compare & Refine

View the original and remixed GIFs side-by-side. Adjust settings and regenerate if needed.

6 Export Your Creation

Happy with the result? Click Export to download your new AI-generated looping GIF!
